# Agoranomos

Open-source marketing BI out of the box: ad platforms → Postgres warehouse → Metabase dashboards →
ask anything in Claude or ChatGPT via MCP. One command. Your data stays yours.

> **Status:** pre-alpha, under active development. Not ready for use yet.

The name comes from the *agoranomoi*, the market overseers of ancient Athens who checked that measures were fair
and goods were what they claimed to be.
