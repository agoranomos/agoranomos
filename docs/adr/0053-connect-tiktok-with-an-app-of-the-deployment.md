# Connect TikTok with an app of the deployment

Supersedes in part [ADR-0038](0038-load-by-copy-and-write-the-core-by-whole-days.md): the window of restatements of TikTok in "The window", 7 days, which becomes 28. The rest of that record stays accepted.

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0032](0032-connect-google-ads-first-with-the-reference-rates-of-the-ecb.md) connected Google Ads first and put TikTok later, because "Each needs an app approved or registered first", and decided that each later source gets a record of its own; [ADR-0050](0050-connect-meta-with-an-app-and-a-system-user-of-the-deployment.md) did so for Meta. Later records already decided parts of the connector of TikTok: [ADR-0034](0034-define-a-basic-data-set-and-the-modules-of-an-extended-one.md) gives every real source a first load of 36 months; [ADR-0035](0035-keep-four-layers-and-erase-the-raw-layer-after-acceptance.md) checks the campaigns of each account against its totals from the platform (G0a), keeps a failed account out of the core, and leaves open the tolerance of G0a, as "TikTok cuts decimal places"; [ADR-0036](0036-model-the-core-as-narrow-facts-and-versioned-dimensions.md) asks the lists of TikTok for `STATUS_ALL`, so that removed entities come too, and a common state beside the status of the platform; [ADR-0038](0038-load-by-copy-and-write-the-core-by-whole-days.md) sets the window of restatements of TikTok to 7 days and the history to portions by quarters, and leaves "The quotas of Meta and TikTok for a short cycle" unverified.

What exists today is the demo: the fictional platform `demo_video` in the shape of the TikTok API for Business v1.3 (`airflow/dags/agoranomos_pipelines/demo/video.py`), the shape macros `dbt/macros/shapes/tiktok_v1_3.sql` that its staging models call, its two conversion actions in `dbt/seeds/seed_conversion_actions.csv`, and its window of 28 days in `dbt/seeds/seed_ad_platforms.csv`, against the 7 of ADR-0038. No connector of TikTok exists.

TikTok differs from Google and Meta in three ways that this record has to settle. It has no system user, so every token carries the rights of a person. Its sign-in hands a code that lives an hour to a redirect URL, and the exchange of the code needs the secret of the app. And on 23 October 2026 it launched a Reporting API v2.0 beside v1.3, whose lists stay on v1.3.

How does a deployment connect TikTok: under which code, with which app and whose token, how is the code exchanged, which advertisers and requests, on which version of the API, with which window, state, checks and quota, and who merges the task that adds its raw schema?

## Decision Drivers

* A deployment connects the source with steps that one person can follow, with the reviews that TikTok requires and no other (ADR-0032).
* The source serves one business: only the advertisers that `extensions/model/clients.toml` names ([ADR-0024](0024-one-deployment-is-one-business.md)).
* The connector fills the model that the demo fills, through the same shape macros, so that the dashboards, the tests and the skills of the demo hold for real data ([ADR-0025](0025-model-advertising-statistics-once.md)).
* AI access and the loads read only: the app asks for read scopes alone, and the person who authorizes it needs no right to change an ad ([ADR-0007](0007-ai-access-via-mcp-read-only.md)).
* As little secret material in `.env` as the sign-in needs, and no secret in a log, in a command line or in the output of `./agoranomos` (AGENTS.md).
* Money keeps its decimals: a cent lost in each row is a loss that G0a cannot see, since the totals would lose it too.
* The daily load and its history fit the limits of the default level of an app.
* The contract of a source: a window in the time zone of the account, safe repeats, explicit retries, loud failures, and an account that fails leaves the others to load ([ADR-0004](0004-ingest-with-dlt.md), ADR-0035).
* The spend of removed entities counts, and G0a sees a read that lost part of an account (ADR-0036, ADR-0035).
* No heavy dependency next to Airflow, and every package pinned (AGENTS.md).

## Considered Options

For the platform code:

* `tiktok_ads`, with the schemas `raw_tiktok_ads` and `raw_tiktok_ads_staging`
* `tiktok`

For the version of the API:

* v1.3 for every request, the reports included
* The reports on the Reporting API v2.0, and the rest on v1.3

For the sunset of v1.3 while TikTok publishes none:

* An estimate for the reports of v1.3, a year after the launch of v2.0, replaced by the date of TikTok once it is published
* No date until TikTok publishes one

For the sign-in, and whose token it is:

* A long-term token of v1.3 from one person who reaches the advertisers, best a member of the Business Center with the role Analyst, or a user kept for the deployment
* A short-term token of v2.0, refreshed every day by the load
* The token of an admin of the Business Center
* One app of the project for every deployment

For the exchange of the code:

* A command of `./agoranomos` that reads the app ID, the secret and the code without echo and writes only the token into `.env`
* A `curl` in the guide
* A callback served by the stack

For the redirect URL of the app:

* `http://localhost/`, with the code copied from the address bar
* A page of the business's own website

For the advertisers that the source loads:

* The advertisers of `tiktok_ads` that `extensions/model/clients.toml` names
* Every advertiser that the token reaches

For the client of the API:

* The HTTP client of dlt, with a pace under the limits of the app and explicit retries
* The SDK of TikTok, `tiktok-business-api-sdk-official`

For the reports:

* Synchronous reports for every request, split into ranges of at most 30 days
* Asynchronous report tasks for the history

For the window of restatements:

* 28 days
* 7 days, as ADR-0038 has it, until C4c sets it from the journal of restatements
* 90 days

For the common state:

* `tiktok_state` over `secondary_status` for removed, and over `operation_status` for the rest
* `tiktok_state` over `operation_status` alone, as the macro has it today

For the tolerance of G0a:

* A share of 0.01 to start with
* Zero, as for Google

For the merge of C5b, which adds the raw schemas:

* The coordinating session may merge it, if its grants copy those of `raw_meta_ads`
* The maintainer merges it

## Decision Outcome

Chosen options: "`tiktok_ads`", "v1.3 for every request", "An estimate for the reports of v1.3, a year after the launch of v2.0", "A long-term token of v1.3 from one person who reaches the advertisers", "A command of `./agoranomos`", "`http://localhost/`", "The advertisers of `tiktok_ads` that `extensions/model/clients.toml` names", "The HTTP client of dlt", "Synchronous reports for every request", "28 days", "`tiktok_state` over `secondary_status` for removed, and over `operation_status` for the rest", "A share of 0.01 to start with" and "The coordinating session may merge it", because the token of a person is the only kind of token that TikTok issues, a command keeps the secret of the app out of `.env` and off a command line, one version serves every request that the connector makes and keeps the shape that `demo_video` already has, and a window of 28 days covers the longest click window of TikTok.

Five of these choices are the maintainer's to accept or change in the review of this record, as #225 asks: the version, the token of a person, the exchange of the code, the window, and the merge of C5b. The text below marks them.

In practice this means:

* **The platform code and the schemas.** The platform is `tiktok_ads`, after `google_ads` and `meta_ads`, with the schemas `raw_tiktok_ads` and `raw_tiktok_ads_staging`, owned by `loader` and read by `transformer` as those of Meta are. The code is in every key of the core and of `extensions/model/clients.toml`, so it never changes once data exists.
* **The app.** The business of the deployment registers as a developer of the TikTok API for Business, with an email of its company's domain, and creates one app with the read scopes alone: Read Ad Account Information (`/advertiser/info/`, `/oauth2/advertiser/get/`), Read Campaigns (`/campaign/get/`), Read Ad Groups (`/adgroup/get/`), Read Ads (`/ad/get/`) and Consolidated Report (`/report/integrated/get/`; the scope also covers the asynchronous reports and the subscriptions of reports, which change no ad). TikTok onboards companies only, reviews a developer in three business days and the app in two to three more, and reviews each change of its scopes again, so the guide of C5b plans about a week before the first load.
* **The sign-in.** *Open to the maintainer's acceptance:* the token is a long-term token of v1.3, which does not expire, from the authorization of one person who reaches the advertisers that `clients.toml` names. The guide recommends a member of the Business Center of the business, or of the agency that the business added as a partner, with the role Analyst on those advertisers, which views ads and reports and creates, edits or pays for nothing. TikTok has no system user: the token carries the rights of that person, so when the person leaves the business or loses the role, the loads of those advertisers fail with the error of permission and a person who still reaches them authorizes the app again. An advertiser that the person gets later is reached by the same token, without a new authorization. The guide also suggests, without requiring it, a TikTok for Business user kept for the deployment, with an address of the company's domain that more than one person can reach, as TikTok itself advises for the developer account, so that the token does not follow one employee; TikTok's terms on such a user were not read (see More Information). The token goes into `.env` as `TIKTOK_ADS_ACCESS_TOKEN` and is sent in the header `Access-Token`, never in a URL, and never appears in a log, an error or the output of `./agoranomos`: the task masks it, and the script checks only its form.
* **The exchange of the code.** *Open to the maintainer's acceptance:* a command of `./agoranomos`, `./agoranomos tiktok connect`, asks for the app ID, the secret and the `auth_code` without echo, calls `POST /open_api/v1.3/oauth2/access_token/` with them, writes only the token into `.env`, and shows which of the advertisers that the answer lists are in `clients.toml` for `tiktok_ads`, and which of those in `clients.toml` the token does not reach. The app ID and the secret stay out of `.env`, as no load needs them. The command replaces a token that `.env` already holds, and says so. C5b builds it, with `docs/reference/command-line.md`.
* **The redirect URL.** The guide sets the advertiser redirect URL of the app to `http://localhost/`. The person who authorizes is sent there with the code in the address, the page fails to load as nothing listens, and the person copies `auth_code` from the address bar into the command, so the code reaches no other host. Two pages of TikTok allow localhost and an FAQ refuses intranet addresses (see More Information); if the form of the app refuses it, the guide falls back to a page of the business's own website, where the code lands in that site's logs, useless without the secret and spent once the command has used it.
* **The advertisers.** The source loads the advertisers of the platform `tiktok_ads` that `extensions/model/clients.toml` names, by their numeric ID, and no other, as for Google and Meta (ADR-0024): a token of a member of an agency reaches the advertisers of other clients too.
* **The version.** *Open to the maintainer's acceptance:* every request goes to v1.3, the reports included. The lists, `/advertiser/info/` and the sign-in exist only on v1.3, since the reference of v2.0 "exclusively documents Reporting endpoints", so one version serves the whole connector. The report of v1.3 takes one advertiser through `advertiser_id`, while v2.0 removed `advertiser_id`, `report_type`, `data_level` and `query_mode`, takes `advertiser_ids` alone, with the ID dimension choosing the level and `filter_values` as an array, and gives `stat_time_day` as `2026-03-02` where v1.3 gives `2020-01-01 00:00:00`. Both say that with several advertisers in one request the money comes "without a decimal point": v1.3 says it of any use of `advertiser_ids`, v2.0 of more than one advertiser in it, and the examples of both show cents for one. The connector therefore sends one advertiser in each report, through `advertiser_id`, never `advertiser_ids`. The answers of v2.0 keep the objects `dimensions` and `metrics` that the macros read, so a move of the reports to v2.0 would change the requests, the dates and the filters, and not the tables of raw.
* **The sunset.** *Open to the maintainer's acceptance:* the version and its sunset are in one place, as for Google and Meta, and a unit test fails from 90 days before the sunset (ADR-0032). TikTok gives v1.3 "TBD", and gave "a year to migrate from v1.2 to v1.3". So, while it says nothing, the sunset of the reports of v1.3 is estimated at a year after the launch of v2.0, 23 October 2027, and the test fails from 25 July 2027. A release replaces the estimate with the date of TikTok once TikTok publishes one. A sunset of the reports of v1.3, estimated or published, brings back the choice of the version, through a record that supersedes this point.
* **The client of the API.** The source calls the API with the HTTP client of dlt (`dlt.sources.helpers.requests`), as `google_ads.py` and `meta_ads.py` do. It paces its calls below 10 a second and 600 a minute for each endpoint, the limits of the level Basic, as "All apps are set to Basic level by default"; a code 40100 waits 5 minutes and asks again within the retries, as TikTok says of its limit by minute; a 5xx waits and asks again, as TikTok advises "about 5 minutes"; and an answer whose `code` is not 0 is an error even with HTTP 200. It does not use the SDK of TikTok.
* **The requests.** For each advertiser, in this order, each request with every page of its answer at `page_size` 1,000:
    * `/advertiser/info/` with `name`, `currency`, `timezone`, `display_timezone` and `status`, which takes the advertisers of a load in one call;
    * the full lists `/campaign/get/`, `/adgroup/get/` and `/ad/get/`, filtered on `primary_status` `STATUS_ALL`, which TikTok defines as "All statuses (including "Deleted")", and on `campaign_system_origins` with both `PROMOTE` and `TT_ADS_PLATFORM`, so that the campaigns made in the TikTok app come too, with `operation_status`, `secondary_status` and the fields that `tiktok_v1_3` reads;
    * the synchronous report `BASIC` by `stat_time_day` at `AUCTION_ADVERTISER`, the totals of G0a, then at `AUCTION_CAMPAIGN`, `AUCTION_ADGROUP` and `AUCTION_AD`, each with the filters `campaign_status`, `adgroup_status` and `ad_status` at `STATUS_ALL`, as each defaults to `STATUS_NOT_DELETE`, with the metrics `spend`, `impressions`, `clicks`, `conversion`, `complete_payment` and `total_complete_payment_rate`, and the names and IDs of the levels above, in ranges of at most 30 days, the longest that a report by day allows.

    A report returns at most 20,000 ads, and TikTok warns of a cut in the header `X-Tt-Ads-Throttle`; beyond that, the report of ads goes by groups of at most 100 ad IDs from the list. A report that times out is not asked again as it was, as TikTok asks, but split: first into shorter ranges, then by groups of at most 30 campaign IDs, the limit that TikTok gives for ad-level reports. TikTok also advises against `STATUS_ALL` in the filters of reports to spare its load; the filters keep it all the same, as ADR-0036 needs the removed entities. No asynchronous report: it "is currently an allowlist-only feature".
* **The conversions.** The two actions of `demo_video`: complete payments, `complete_payment`, with their value in `total_complete_payment_rate`, primary, and the optimization event, `conversion`, not primary. Both are metrics of each row of the three levels at no extra request, and the core keeps the conversions by action of campaigns only until the module of C13, as for Google and Meta.
* **The window and the history.** *Open to the maintainer's acceptance:* the window of restatements of `tiktok_ads` is 28 days, so a daily load of an advertiser takes `[min(yesterday + 1 − 28, last accepted day + 1), yesterday]` in its time zone (ADR-0038), one request a level a day. TikTok dates a conversion by the day of the ad interaction and allows a click window of up to 28 days, so the conversions of a day can grow for 28 days after it, while a window of 7 would freeze them at a week. C4c (#224) can shorten or widen the window from the journal of restatements once the stand has run. The daily load runs after the data fix of TikTok, at 12:00 UTC or later, as one page names 12:00 and another 8:00. The history goes back to the first day of the month 36 months before yesterday (ADR-0034), by quarters from new to old through `history.py`, each quarter in requests of at most 30 days; TikTok keeps report data "forever", so no oldest day bounds it.
* **The time zone.** The core takes the time zone of an advertiser from `timezone`, as `tiktok_accounts` does, and raw keeps `display_timezone` beside it: TikTok documents `timezone` as a name with a GMT offset or a region, such as `Etc/GMT+8`, and `display_timezone` as a region, such as `America/Los_Angeles`, and says only that the dates of a report are "based on the ad account time zone".
* **The common state.** `tiktok_state` gives removed when `secondary_status` is one of the values of a deletion, `CAMPAIGN_STATUS_DELETE`, `ADGROUP_STATUS_DELETE`, `ADGROUP_STATUS_CAMPAIGN_DELETE`, `AD_STATUS_DELETE`, `AD_STATUS_CAMPAIGN_DELETE` and `AD_STATUS_ADGROUP_DELETE`, named one by one, as the filter values `*_STATUS_NOT_DELETE` end in `_DELETE` too; otherwise `operation_status` `ENABLE` is active, `DISABLE` paused, and anything else, `FROZEN` among them, other. The macro today maps an `operation_status` `DELETE` that TikTok does not send, as `operation_status` takes `ENABLE`, `DISABLE` and, below the campaign, `FROZEN`. The lists return no `primary_status`, so the state reads `secondary_status`. An ad group or an ad of a deleted campaign is removed too, as TikTok gives it a status of that deletion, such as `ADGROUP_STATUS_CAMPAIGN_DELETE`, while one of a paused campaign keeps its own state. C5d changes the macro, and `demo_video` sends `secondary_status` from then on.
* **G0a.** The totals of `AUCTION_ADVERTISER`, which return "data of all Auction Ads and Reservation Ads under the advertiser account", against the campaigns with `STATUS_ALL`. `platform_totals_tolerance.tiktok_ads` starts at 0.01, as for Meta, since the spend of each row comes as a string with the decimals of its currency, and the rounding of each campaign may not sum to that of the advertiser. The value is unverified and may be wrong both ways, as ADR-0050 says of Meta; the stand S6 (#220) measures it.
* **The quota.** The limits of an app are per endpoint: 10 a second, 600 a minute and 864,000 a day at Basic, reset at 00:00 UTC. A daily load costs one call of `/advertiser/info/` for the advertisers of a load, and for each advertiser three lists and four reports, with one more call for each further page: about 700 calls a day and their further pages for one hundred advertisers, of which 400 go to the report endpoint, which the pace spreads over a minute or more. A quarter of history costs, for each advertiser, four ranges a level, so sixteen reports and their pages. The short cycle of [ADR-0051](0051-take-the-current-day-as-a-load-of-the-kind-intraday.md) would cost one report a cycle for each advertiser and fits by these numbers, which settles row 21 of the register (#225) for TikTok on paper; it is not part of v0.7. TikTok publishes no limit by advertiser.
* **The IDs.** TikTok gives every ID as a string since v1.3, unique across the platform; the core keeps them as `bigint` (ADR-0036), through a cast in staging that fails loudly on a value that does not fit, which G1 then reports as a form error. TikTok does not document their length.
* **The merge of C5b.** *Open to the maintainer's acceptance:* C5b adds the raw schemas `raw_tiktok_ads` and `raw_tiktok_ads_staging` to `postgres/warehouse.sql`. If their owner and grants copy those of `raw_meta_ads`, `loader` owning and `transformer` reading, and no role gains a right on another object, the coordinating session may merge C5b, as the maintainer allowed for C1b, C2a and C2b. Any other change of a role or a grant leaves the merge to the maintainer.

The other options were not chosen for these reasons:

* **`tiktok`** would leave no room for the other APIs of TikTok that a deployment may load later, such as the organic accounts of the Accounts API, and break the form `<platform>_ads` of the two connectors before it.
* **The reports on v2.0, and the rest on v1.3** puts one connector on two versions, one of them launched a month before this record, with a date format and filters of its own, for no data that v1.3 lacks. It stays the way out when v1.3 is to end, once a check on a live advertiser shows that a report of one advertiser on v2.0 keeps its decimals.
* **No date until TikTok publishes one** gives no warning while the date is "TBD", although TikTok has a new version of the reports and a precedent of a year to migrate.
* **A short-term token of v2.0** expires in 24 hours and its refresh token in 365 days after each refresh, so the load would refresh it every day with the app secret, which `.env` would then hold, and keep both tokens in the database of Airflow; and it carries the rights of a person all the same, through a sign-in of a version that this connector does not otherwise use.
* **The token of an admin of the Business Center** grants the rights to edit ads, pay and manage members to whoever reads `.env`, which the loads never use. TikTok asks for an admin to authorize the Business Center API, which this connector does not call.
* **One app of the project for every deployment** would make the project hold one secret for every deployment, which each exchange of a code needs, against the rule of ADR-0032 of an app of each deployment.
* **A `curl` in the guide** puts the secret on a command line, in the history of the shell and in the list of processes, and the token in the output of the terminal, which a person then copies into `.env` by hand.
* **A callback served by the stack** needs a port that server mode does not open ([ADR-0019](0019-open-only-metabase-in-server-mode.md)) and, by the FAQ of TikTok, an address on the Internet, for a step that a person does once.
* **A page of the business's own website** as the first choice sends each code to a host that the deployment does not run.
* **Every advertiser that the token reaches** would bring in the advertisers of other clients of an agency, against ADR-0024.
* **The SDK of TikTok** 1.1.3 (27 February 2026) is "automatically generated by the Swagger Codegen project", brings `urllib3`, `six`, `certifi` and `python-dateutil`, and returns objects rather than the JSON that dlt writes as the shape macros read it.
* **Asynchronous report tasks** are allowlist-only, so a deployment cannot count on them.
* **7 days** would freeze the conversions of each day at a week, while clicks are attributed for up to 28, and C4c could only learn of it from restatements that the window never sees. **90 days** would cost three requests a level a day and rewrite 90 days of the core a day, for the in-app conversions of app campaigns, which TikTok attributes for up to 90 days after an install; C4c can widen the window for them once the journal shows a need.
* **`tiktok_state` over `operation_status` alone** never gives removed, as TikTok does not send `DELETE` there.
* **Zero tolerance** would put advertisers in quarantine for rounding.
* **The maintainer merges C5b** is the rule for a change of the roles and grants of the database; the new schemas only copy grants that the maintainer let the coordinating session merge for C1b, C2a and C2b, so the condition above keeps the rule for every other change while sparing the wait.

### Consequences

* Good, because the app reads with read scopes alone, and the token of an Analyst can change nothing in the ad accounts.
* Good, because `.env` holds one secret, the token, and the secret of the app never reaches a file, a log or a command line of the deployment.
* Good, because the connector and `demo_video` share the shape macros, so the dashboards, the tests and the skills of the demo hold for real data.
* Good, because one advertiser in each report keeps the decimals of money, and G0a asks the level of the advertiser, so a list or a filter that misses deleted entities shows as `totals_differ`, not as lost spend.
* Good, because a window of 28 days covers the longest click window of TikTok, so the conversions of a day settle within the window.
* Bad, because TikTok has no system user: the token follows a person, and when that person leaves or loses the role, the loads fail until someone authorizes the app again.
* Bad, because a token that never expires is a long-lived secret: whoever reads `.env` or a copy of it reads every advertiser that the person reaches, those of other clients of an agency included, until the authorization is cancelled.
* Bad, because a deployment waits about a week for the reviews of TikTok, and a business without a company domain cannot register at all.
* Bad, because the sunset of the reports of v1.3 is an estimate, and a release has to move it when TikTok publishes a date, or move the reports to v2.0.
* Bad, because a window of 28 days costs a write of 28 days of the core for each advertiser every day, four times that of 7.
* Bad, because a tolerance of 0.01 can quarantine a small day for rounding and pass a loss of less than 1%, until S6 measures it.

## More Information

* **Implementation status, as of November 2026:** partly built. The maintainer accepted the record on 22 November 2026. C5b of #225 (#379) built the load into raw and the command of the code, `./agoranomos tiktok connect`, and C5d the core and the marts: the staging models of `dbt/models/staging/tiktok_ads/` over the shape macros `tiktok_v1_3`, `tiktok_state` over `secondary_status` and `operation_status`, the two conversion actions in the seed, G0a against `report_advertiser_daily` with `platform_totals_tolerance.tiktok_ads` at 0.01, and the IDs cast to `bigint`. A cast in staging cannot both fail and leave G1 to report, as "The IDs" says, so the maintainer chose on 23 November 2026 that G1 reports: an ID that is not a whole number that fits `bigint` is never cut, staging leaves it out, and G1 holds its advertiser in quarantine with the reason `invalid_id`, while the other advertisers go on. C5c, the history by quarters, comes in a pull request of its own, #380. No load has reached a real TikTok advertiser.
* **Not checked yet.** The stand S6 (#220) settles each of these on a live advertiser:
    * Row 19 of the register (#225), removed entities: that the lists with `primary_status` `STATUS_ALL` and the reports with the three status filters at `STATUS_ALL` return deleted entities, and that the campaigns add up to the totals of the advertiser. The pages say that `AUCTION_ADVERTISER` returns the data of all ads of the account and name the default filter of deleted entities only for the three lower levels, but do not say in so many words that its totals count deleted campaigns.
    * Row 27 of the register (#225), the tolerance of G0a, and the decimals of each currency of the agency in the reports of one advertiser.
    * Row 21, the quota: the pages of a request, the throttling over a week of daily loads and a portion of history.
    * The redirect URL on `localhost`: the page of authorization of v1.3 and the page of the app of v2.0 allow up to 10 redirect URLs "including localhost", while the FAQ of authorization of v1.3 says "The callback address must be an extranet address" and that an intranet address, such as `https://localhost:port_number/`, does not work.
    * The life of `auth_code`: "only valid for one hour" in the guides and the FAQ table, and "only valid for 10 minutes" in an error of the same FAQ. The guide of C5b tells the person to run the command at once.
    * Whether an exchange of a new code invalidates the token that the same app had before: an error of the FAQ of authentication reads "when you call access_token or refresh_token api, old token will become invalid".
    * The hour of the data fix, 12:00 UTC by the page of latency and 8:00 UTC by the FAQ of reporting.
    * Which of `timezone` and `display_timezone` the days of a report follow, where they differ, as in the example `Etc/GMT+8` and `America/Los_Angeles`.
    * Whether the reports include the campaigns made in the TikTok app ("Promote"), which the lists leave out by default.
    * How long the conversions of a day change, from the journal of restatements, which C4c reads.
