# Take the current day as a load of the kind intraday in the one transform

## Context and Problem Statement

[ADR-0038](0038-load-by-copy-and-write-the-core-by-whole-days.md) ("The current day") takes the current day of each account in a short cycle, at the level of campaigns, with `is_partial_day` on the row, every 3 hours by default. It builds the cycle as "a separate light DAG" with "its own table in L0" that "runs `dbt build --select tag:intraday`", and says that "both cycles write one fact, so both go through an Airflow pool of one slot, where they queue and do not collide". [ADR-0046](0046-pin-the-loads-before-the-transform-and-accept-them-before-the-cleanup.md) has staging read, for each account and day, "the load with the largest `_dlt_load_id`".

Neither sentence holds against the code of November 2026:

* **A pool serializes tasks, not runs.** The transform runs in `ExecutionMode.LOCAL` with `TestBehavior.BUILD`, one task of dbt for each node, in the pool `dbt` of two slots (`airflow/dags/agoranomos_demo_transform.py`), and the maintainer decided on 12 November 2026 that the watcher mode of [ADR-0039](0039-run-one-dbt-build-in-the-watcher-mode.md) waits for the measurements of the long-running stand ([#203](https://github.com/agoranomos/agoranomos/issues/203)). A run fills the tables `staging.stg_loads__newest_days`, `staging.int_window_days` and `staging.int_g1_failures` once, and its later tasks read them. A light build that queued in the same pool would rebuild them between two tasks of a daily run, which would then write its facts from the days of the light one.
* **A light run cannot accept by itself.** The pin, the acceptance and the cleanup are tasks of the DAG of the transform, outside dbt. The acceptance marks as accepted every pinned row of `core.loads` up to the newest `pinned_at` that the run read, of any source and any DAG (`ACCEPT` in `airflow/dags/agoranomos_pipelines/acceptance.py`). A light run that pinned and accepted would accept the daily loads that it never wrote, and the cleanup would delete their rows from L0. A separate path would need a pin, days, checks and an acceptance of its own, and every staging model of the facts joins `stg_loads__newest_days`, so the fork would reach all of staging. The watcher mode does not change this: the pin and the acceptance stay outside the producer.
* **The largest load ID can lose a whole day.** A read of the current day that started before midnight in the account and reached dlt after the daily load had begun would get the larger `_dlt_load_id`, and replace the whole day with a partial one.

The design of the code has one transform DAG with `max_active_runs = 1`, which already keeps any two runs of the transform apart, and pins, reads, accepts and cleans every load in one way. How does the short cycle enter it, how does a partial day stay below a whole one, and what must leave it out?

## Decision Drivers

* One run of the transform reads one set of loads and accepts only what it read ([ADR-0046](0046-pin-the-loads-before-the-transform-and-accept-them-before-the-cleanup.md)).
* A partial day never replaces a whole one, and its replacement by a whole one is no restatement ([ADR-0038](0038-load-by-copy-and-write-the-core-by-whole-days.md)).
* A stop of any length still closes by itself: the watermark counts whole days only.
* The checks and the tests of the transform, and the rates of the day, apply to today as to any day ([ADR-0035](0035-keep-four-layers-and-erase-the-raw-layer-after-acceptance.md)).
* It works in the local mode of today, and in the watcher mode without a change.
* No new service, so the memory budgets of [ADR-0022](0022-budget-the-memory-by-variant-and-mode.md) and [ADR-0048](0048-count-the-one-shot-jobs-that-can-run-at-the-same-time.md) stay.

## Considered Options

* A load of the kind `intraday` into the raw tables of its source, which the one transform takes like any load
* A separate light DAG with its own table in L0 and a pool of one slot for dbt, as ADR-0038 says
* The same light DAG after the watcher mode, with a pin and an acceptance of its own
* A `dbt run-operation` that writes the current day into the fact outside the models

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "A load of the kind `intraday`", because it adds no second path through the pin, the acceptance and the cleanup: the rules of ADR-0046 stay one, `max_active_runs = 1` of the transform keeps the two cycles apart in the local mode and in the watcher mode, and every check and test of a run applies to today. What it costs is a full run of the transform for each cycle, which the consequences count.

In practice this means:

* **A load of a new kind.** A load of the short cycle has the kind `intraday` in the journal `_agoranomos_loads` of its source and in `core.loads`, beside `window` and `history`. It writes into the same raw tables, under its own `_dlt_load_id`, with the window of one day, the current day of each account in its time zone at the moment of the read. It brings impressions, clicks and cost of the campaigns only: no lists of entities, no totals of the account, no ad groups, ads or conversions by action. Its conversions are none (NULL), not zero, as Google dates them by click and adds them later. A DAG of its own updates an asset of its own in the condition `LOADED` of the transform (`airflow/dags/agoranomos_pipelines/assets.py`), so that the transform starts after it and the watermark of the asset of the daily load stays the daily one's. The transform pins, reads, accepts and cleans it like any load, and a load that ends during a run waits for the next one, as ADR-0046 has it.
* **A whole day beats a partial one.** For each account and day, staging takes the newest load of the kinds `window` and `history` that covers the day, and a load of the kind `intraday` only for a day that no such load covers, whatever their load IDs. Among the loads of one rank, the largest load ID wins, as before. The rule holds against an accepted whole load too: the bound of ADR-0046 on the accepted loads, those newer than the oldest pinned load of the account (#191), counts for the loads of one rank, and an accepted whole load that covers a day of a pinned intraday load keeps the day, which is then not read. The rule goes into `pinned_loads` of `dbt/macros/newest_load.sql`, and the pin copies the kind from the journal into `core.loads` (`dbt/macros/pin_loads.sql`, whose `CASE` turns every kind but `history` into `window` today).
* **`is_partial_day`.** A migration adds the column `is_partial_day boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false` to `core.ad_stats_campaign_daily`, and widens the CHECK of `core.loads.kind` to `intraday`, as the core changes by addition ([ADR-0044](0044-keep-the-core-for-good-and-change-it-by-migrations.md)). The fact writes it from the kind of the load of the day. A day of an intraday load is a day of the fact of campaigns only, so no other fact gets a partial row, and no other fact reads the day as an empty day and deletes it (`fact_days` and `fact_empty_days` in `dbt/macros/facts.sql`). A whole load replaces a partial day within the whole-day writes of ADR-0038: `delete+insert` by account and day deletes the partial rows with the day, and inserts the whole ones with `is_partial_day = false`. The freeze of ADR-0035 is never involved, as the current day lies in the newest month. The view of campaigns carries the column as the service column of [ADR-0037](0037-serve-the-marts-as-views-over-the-core.md).
* **What leaves intraday loads out.**
    * The journal of restatements of [ADR-0036](0036-model-the-core-as-narrow-facts-and-versioned-dimensions.md) skips an old row with `is_partial_day`, so the replacement of a partial day is no restatement.
    * G1a of ADR-0035 skips intraday loads and leaves partial rows out of what the core holds: a partial day is below the day that it becomes, so the check would hold an account on the first cycle of each day, and with it every load that the account has pinned, its daily window included.
    * The lists of entities: `newest_lists` leaves intraday loads out, as they take no lists. A run that held only intraday loads would otherwise read empty lists and mark every campaign as no longer listed.
    * Every watermark: each reader of `window_to` or `as_of` in the journals and in `core.loads` leaves the kind `intraday` out. These are `accepted()`, `last_day()`, `known_windows()` and `plan()` of `airflow/dags/agoranomos_pipelines/google_ads.py`, `recorded()` of `loads.py`, which the demo and the rates use, the `due()` of the daily load of the demo, which skips the load when every account has a load of its day, the late accounts of `watcher.py`, `dbt/models/ops/ops_loads.sql`, and the retention of `core.loads` in `maintenance.py`. Without it, after a stop of 120 days, an accepted intraday load of the first day back would set the last accepted day to today, the next daily window would start 90 days back, and 30 days would be lost for good; and a new account with an intraday load only would look to the history of Google Ads as an account that lacks every day before today. The DAG of maintenance deletes the accepted intraday rows of `core.loads` that no fact reads once they are 14 days old, so that they do not stay for good: 14 days is a proposal for the maintainer.
    * G0a of ADR-0035 does not check an intraday load, as the code stands: it compares a load with the totals of the account in the same load and skips a load without totals (`quarantine_totals_findings` in `dbt/macros/quarantine.sql`), and the short cycle asks for none. G1 applies as to any load.
* **One load of a source at a time.** The loads of one source go through an Airflow pool of one slot: `google_ads_load` for the daily load of Google Ads, its runs of the history and its short cycle, and `demo_load`, as today, for the loads of the demo. `airflow-init` sets the new pool on every start, as it does `dbt` and `demo_load` (`compose.yaml`). Today `max_active_runs = 1` of the DAG of Google Ads keeps its daily load and its runs of the history apart, but a DAG of the short cycle is another DAG. This is the "pool with one slot" of ADR-0038, put on the loads and not on dbt, where the transform keeps its pool `dbt`.
* **The period.** A setting in `.env` gives the period of the short cycle in hours, a divisor of 24 (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 or 24), at a fixed minute in UTC, or 0 for off, with 3 by default, as ADR-0038 says. `./agoranomos` refuses another value, as it does for its other settings. A run that waited longer than the period ends without a load, as the next one comes.
* **The day before, after midnight.** While no whole load covers the day before in the account, the short cycle takes it too, in the same request, so that between midnight in the account and the daily load (10:37 UTC for Google Ads) the day before goes on growing instead of keeping the value of its last cycle. It costs no operation of the quota. This bullet is open to the acceptance of the maintainer; without it, the short cycle takes only the current day, as ADR-0038 says.
* **Partial days in the numbers.** A period, a KPI, a comparison of periods, a week and a month take closed days only, as ADR-0038 says, and so do the metrics of the glossary of Metabase (`metabase/metrics.py`, [ADR-0043](0043-define-the-metrics-in-the-warehouse-and-give-agents-tools-over-the-marts.md)), so that a metric and the card of the same period give one number. A card "Today, as of the load" answers the partial rows, with the time of the newest load. This bullet is open to the acceptance of the maintainer; the other choice is a metric that counts today as spent so far, and says so in its description.
* **The profiles.** The short cycle is on, every 3 hours, in the default profile and in the demo, and the block of the agency profile in `.env.example` turns it off, until C3c of [#223](https://github.com/agoranomos/agoranomos/issues/223) has measured a cycle on that profile and the watcher mode has lowered its cost. This bullet is open to the acceptance of the maintainer; the other choices are 3 hours there too, or a longer period.
* **Meta and the other platforms.** The design takes any source, with a pool of its own. The short cycle of Meta waits for its quotas, which depend on the level of access (row 21 of the register of the audit, and "Not checked yet" of ADR-0038), in a task after that check and not in the connector of Meta. This bullet is open to the acceptance of the maintainer.

The other options were not chosen for these reasons:

* **The light DAG of ADR-0038** fails both ways described above: in the local mode its build rebuilds the tables of a daily run between two of its tasks, and its acceptance, by itself, accepts what the daily run pinned and lets the cleanup delete it. Its own table in L0 would also need its own staging, as every staging model of the facts joins the days of the pin.
* **The light DAG after the watcher mode** removes the first failure and keeps the second: a light run still needs its own pin, its own days, its own checks and its own acceptance, a second path through the rules of ADR-0046. It also waits for the watcher mode, which waits for the measurements of the stand.
* **A run-operation of dbt** would write the fact outside the models: no contract, no test of dbt, no G1 or G2, and no rate of the current day, which `core_fx_rates_daily` writes up to the last day of the windows of a run. It would also be a write into the core that no pin or acceptance records.

### Consequences

* Good, because the pin, the acceptance and the cleanup keep one rule for every load, and the two cycles cannot collide: the transform runs one run at a time, and a load that ends during a run waits for the next one.
* Good, because it works in the local mode of today, and the watcher mode changes its cost, not its design: the producer keeps the pool `dbt`, and `max_active_runs` keeps the runs apart.
* Good, because the checks and the tests of a run, and the rate of the day, apply to today.
* Good, because a partial day cannot replace a whole one, whatever the order in which the loads end.
* Bad, because in the local mode each cycle is a full run of the transform, as dear in orchestration as a daily run, however few rows it writes. A run took 160 s for the demo on a Mac (ADR-0039, October 2026) and 239 s on a project of the size of the demo over empty tables, on a Mac (R1, 1 November 2026), and 789 to 812 s on the test server of B16 (11 and 12 November 2026). Eight cycles a day are 21 to 32 minutes of the transform on a Mac, and about 1.8 hours on that server. The watcher mode would cut it: R1 measured 36 and 39 s at the size of the demo.
* Bad, because a daily run of the transform may wait behind a run of the short cycle, up to the length of a run: about 13 minutes on the test server of B16.
* Bad, because the warehouse runs eight more transforms a day, in the hours when people read; the writes of today are small (0.2 + 0.07 s for 6,000 rows of campaigns at the density of the limit, ADR-0038), but the rest of a run, the dimensions, the snapshots, the tests and elementary, is not measured beside the readers.
* Bad, because elementary writes rows on every run: at about 1.1 MB a run, as measured in v0.4 ([ADR-0044](0044-keep-the-core-for-good-and-change-it-by-migrations.md)), eight more runs a day come to about 800 MB over the 90 days that the maintenance keeps.
* Bad, because a cycle costs quota: two operations of Google Ads for each account, the customer, for its time zone, and the statistics of today, so 16 a day at 3 hours, beside the 12 of a daily load. Explorer access, 2,880 operations a day, then carries about 100 accounts instead of about 240, and an agency of 200 accounts with the short cycle needs Basic access, 15,000 a day (read on 14 November 2026). A time zone read from the dimension of accounts would halve the cost of a cycle.
* Bad, because every reader of a watermark has to know the third kind, and one that does not closes a stop that never closed; the unit tests of each reader carry the case of a stop of 120 days.
* Bad, because the choice of the newest load gains a rank of the kind, and the bound on the accepted loads gains the case of an older whole load.
* Neutral: two DAGs of the short cycle, for Google Ads and the demo, a pool, a column, a kind and a setting; no service and no new right of a role.

### Confirmation

Stack tests write a load of the kind `intraday` into the raw tables of the demo and its journal, as `tests/stack/test_core_facts.py` writes a changed day, and check that a run of the transform writes today into the fact of campaigns with `is_partial_day` and into no other fact or journal; that a later whole load replaces it with no row of the journal; that an intraday load with a larger load ID than a whole one leaves the day whole; that a run with only an intraday load leaves the lists and the dimensions as they were; and that a partial day below what the core holds puts no account in quarantine. Unit tests check that each reader of a watermark leaves the kind out, with the case of a stop of 120 days. A stack test of the demo runs the whole cycle. The code review checks that no reader of `window_to` or `as_of` counts an intraday load.
