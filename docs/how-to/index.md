# How-to guides

How-to guides are short recipes for people who already have Agoranomos running and want to get a specific job done. Each guide starts from a goal and lists the steps to reach it. For background and reasoning, see [Explanation](../explanation/index.md).

## Planned guides

- **Add a data source**: connect another ad platform, analytics or CRM source, or load files.
- **Upgrade**: move to a newer release of Agoranomos.
- **Back up and restore**: protect your data and bring it back after a failure.
- **Connect an AI client**: give Claude, ChatGPT or a coding agent access to your data through MCP.

Guides are published together with the features they describe. Agoranomos is pre-alpha and not ready for use yet, so there is nothing to follow today.
