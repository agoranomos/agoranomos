# See the demo

Agoranomos comes with a demo: the history of 18 months of a fictional business with two clients, on four fictional platforms in the shapes of real APIs, with a fictional source of exchange rates. On the first start the demo data goes all the way through the stack, into the model of advertising statistics ([The data model](../reference/data-model.md)), so you can see what each part does before you connect a real ad platform.

## What happens on the first start

1. `./agoranomos up` starts the stack, and the one-shot job `demo-init` starts once Airflow and Metabase are ready.
2. `demo-init` unpauses the DAGs `agoranomos_demo_history` and `agoranomos_demo_transform` in Airflow and runs the history: its five tasks, one after another, load 18 months up to yesterday, in the time zone of each account, into the schemas `raw_demo_search`, `raw_demo_social`, `raw_demo_video` and `raw_demo_web` of the warehouse, in the shapes of the Google Ads API v25, the Meta Marketing API v26.0, the TikTok API for Business v1.3 and the GA4 Data API v1beta, and the exchange rates into `raw_demo_fx`.
3. Each load updates the asset of the main table of its platform, and the DAG `agoranomos_demo_transform` runs when all five have loaded. Its first task loads the settings of the model: the clients of the demo, and the files of the deployment in `extensions/model/` ([Configuration](../reference/configuration.md#the-settings-of-the-model)). Then dbt, through Cosmos, builds the model of advertising statistics ([ADR-0025](../adr/0025-model-advertising-statistics-once.md)): the seeds, the staging views of each platform, the steps that the platforms share, and the marts, such as `marts.ad_stats_campaign_daily`. The tests of each model run right after it, and a failed test stops the models that read it.
4. `demo-init` syncs the warehouse in Metabase, applies the metadata of the marts, and creates the collection **Agoranomos**, with the collection **Demo** in it, its three questions and the dashboard **Ad spend (demo)**, whose questions read `marts.ad_stats_campaign_daily`. Every group but Administrators gets at most View on them.

`up` waits for `demo-init`, which gives up after 10 minutes. When it fails, `up` still waits for the rest of the stack, prints the addresses of Airflow and Metabase, says that the stack is up and the demo is not ready, and exits with 1. `./agoranomos logs demo-init` shows one line that names the step that failed or ran out of time, and for a step of the pipeline the DAG to look at in Airflow. Fix the cause and run `./agoranomos up` again: the next start does what is not done yet.

## Look at it

- **Metabase**, at the address that `up` prints: sign in with `AGORANOMOS_ADMIN_EMAIL` and `METABASE_ADMIN_PASSWORD` from `.env` (`./agoranomos credentials` shows the address, the email and the setting that holds the password), and open the collection **Agoranomos / Demo**. The dashboard shows the spend of the last seven days that have data, in total and by campaign, and the spend of every day, in the reporting currency, `AGORANOMOS_REPORTING_CURRENCY` of `.env` (USD by default). The two charts use the colors of the project.
- **Airflow**, at the address that `up` prints: sign in as `admin` with `AIRFLOW_ADMIN_PASSWORD` from `.env`, and open the DAGs `agoranomos_demo_history`, whose five tasks load one platform each, and `agoranomos_demo_transform`, their runs and the assets between them.

## What later starts do

`demo-init` runs on every start, and each step looks at what is there first.

- Once a run of `agoranomos_demo_history` has succeeded and a run of `agoranomos_demo_transform` has succeeded after it, the pipeline does not run again, and `demo-init` leaves both DAGs as they are: if you pause them, they stay paused. A history without a good transform after it, as after a failed critical test, runs again on the next start, which replaces the same days. A stack of v0.1, whose transform ran before the history existed, runs both on its first start of this release, which also removes the demo of v0.1 from the warehouse ([Upgrading from v0.1](../reference/data-model.md#upgrading-from-v01)).
- The demo is view-only for everyone but admins: on every start, `metabase-init`, and `demo-init` after it, give every group but Administrators at most View on **Agoranomos** and on every collection in it, **Demo** included, and `demo-init` does the same for each collection that it makes, right after it makes it ([ADR-0018](../adr/0018-keep-the-content-of-the-package-in-metabase-as-code.md)). So no account but an admin can put anything into them; should anything reach a new collection before that, `demo-init` puts the collection in the trash with it and stops with one line, and the next start makes the collection again.
- Each start of `demo-init` puts back what differs from the code: the names and descriptions of the collections, the questions and the dashboard, where they are, the type and the whole query of each question, all its settings of display, such as the titles of the columns and the color of the charts, its filters, and the metadata of the columns of its answer, whose display names and descriptions people and AI clients read, and each card of the dashboard, whole: its question, its place, its settings, such as a click behavior, and its filter mappings, with the filters and the tabs of the dashboard. A setting that someone added is put back too. It brings an item back from the trash, creates again one that was deleted for good, and logs each change, with the value that it replaced for a name or a description, which `./agoranomos logs demo-init` shows; Metabase keeps the earlier version in the history of revisions of the item. It finds them by the entity IDs that Metabase gave them, not by name: a renamed question is still the question of the demo and gets its name back, and a collection of the same name that someone else made is left alone, as is anything else that an admin puts into **Agoranomos** or **Demo**. An admin's edit holds until the next start.
- To keep a variant of your own, duplicate the dashboard, with copies of its questions, into a collection of your own, and change the copy. The questions are SQL, so changing the query of a copy needs the permission of native queries.
- The demo collection of v0.1, **Agoranomos demo** at the root, moves into **Agoranomos** as **Demo** at the first start of v0.2.0, once. On a Metabase of v0.1 every account may make a collection at the root, so the name alone proves nothing: the demo of v0.1 is the collection of that name that holds the dashboard **Ad spend (demo)** that the account of `demo-init` made, the lowest by its ID if there are several. It keeps that dashboard, so the links to it keep working, and the questions of v0.1 that the same account made, which are put right as the code says. Everything else in it, such as a question that you added or renamed, a sub-collection, a timeline with its events, or anything that another account made, goes to the root first, also what is in the trash from it, which stays in the trash, with one line for each in `./agoranomos logs demo-init` by its kind and its ID, so that only the content of the package enters **Agoranomos**. Before it moves, it is locked at the root, so that no group but Administrators may curate it any more, and it must hold nothing else then: should anything have reached it before the lock, `demo-init` stops with one line that names the item by its kind and its ID, the collection stays at the root, view-only, and the next start takes that item out and moves the collection. A collection of that name that is not the demo stays where it is, and one line says so; so does the collection of v0.1 when it holds an item of a kind that `demo-init` does not take out, such as a document, also one in the trash, or a pulse. In both cases the demo is made anew. Copies of the questions in other collections are not touched. To stop all of this, turn the demo off.

## Bring the demo up to date

The demo data ends on the day before the first start: the history covers the 18 months up to that day, and the demo has no daily load in this release. To add the days since, trigger `agoranomos_demo_history` in Airflow. It loads the 18 months up to yesterday again and replaces the days of each account that it covers, so no row is duplicated, and the transform follows when all five platforms have loaded.

## Turn the demo off

Set `COMPOSE_PROFILES=core` in `.env` before the first start, and `up` makes no demo: the demo DAGs stay paused in Airflow. After the first start the same setting only stops `demo-init`, whose container from its last run stays in `./agoranomos status`. To remove that container, run `./agoranomos down` once with the demo profile on, with `COMPOSE_PROFILES=core,demo` in `.env`: for example just before you set `COMPOSE_PROFILES=core`. The demo data stays in the warehouse and the collection **Agoranomos / Demo** stays in Metabase, view-only, as `metabase-init` keeps it on every start; an admin can move it to the trash there. If you turn the demo on again, the next start brings it back from the trash.

`./agoranomos init` never changes a setting that is in `.env` already, so a `.env` from before the demo keeps `COMPOSE_PROFILES=core`. To turn the demo on there, set `COMPOSE_PROFILES=core,demo`.
